I am writing to apply for the Cybersecurity Internship at Hilti, with a particular interest in the Offensive Security track. I would welcome the chance to bring this offensive-security and application-security background to your team, contribute to real projects from day one, and keep learning from experienced mentors across your cybersecurity program.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my experience and motivation further.
